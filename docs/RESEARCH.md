# Research and distinct scope — November 1, 2026

Reports cross-time-zone collaboration and after-hours meetings. This motivates a small working-hours planning tool; no productivity or health benefit is asserted.

Apple Foundation Calendar: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/calendar
Platform date components and time-zone conversion are used instead of fixed UTC offsets. Documentation is referenced, not copied. Apple SDKs are installed platform tools, not redistributed.
